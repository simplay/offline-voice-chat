The moon rose over a quiet garden. A little hedgehog was looking for a place to sleep.

A rabbit showed him a warm pile of leaves beneath the apple tree. "This is my favorite spot," she said.

The hedgehog thanked her and curled up in the leaves. The rabbit hopped home, and the stars came out one by one.

The garden grew still. Under the apple tree, the hedgehog dreamed of sunshine and sweet apples. Goodnight, little hedgehog.
